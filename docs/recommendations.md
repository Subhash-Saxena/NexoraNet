# NexoraNet — Pedagogical Recommendation & Achievement Engine

**“Learn. Simulate. Analyze. Defend.”**

## 1. Recommendation Philosophy
The recommendation engine analyzes skill confidence, accuracy, and recency to identify educational opportunities. Rather than judging the student, recommendations suggest targeted practice to build confidence or reinforce recently learned topics.

### Neutral Phrasing Guidelines:
- Recommendation rationale is grounded in evidence counts and topic relevance.
- Example: *"Recent results suggest additional practice with subnetting may be useful."*
- Unlocked recommendations provide direct action URLs into Labs, Challenges, or Lessons.

## 2. 10 Educational Achievements
The platform recognizes 10 milestones across conceptual and hands-on modules:
1. `FIRST_LESSON`: First Step — Completed your first curriculum instructional lesson.
2. `LAB_PIONEER`: Sandbox Pioneer — Completed your first hands-on networking sandbox lab.
3. `EXAM_READY`: Examination Ready — Completed a full 30-question diagnostic mock exam sitting.
4. `PACKET_DISSECTOR`: Packet Dissector — Investigated and extracted evidence from a synthetic PCAP.
5. `TRIAGE_SPECIALIST`: Triage Specialist — Triaged an incoming security event in the Mini-SOC.
6. `THREAT_HUNTER`: Threat Hunter — Formulated a hypothesis and queried security events.
7. `SIEM_OPERATOR`: SIEM Operator — Investigated an authentication spike using log queries.
8. `ENDPOINT_DEFENDER`: Endpoint Defender — Traced a parent-child process tree anomaly.
9. `INCIDENT_RESPONDER`: Incident Responder — Executed a simulated containment and eradication action.
10. `AUTOMATION_ENGINEER`: Automation Engineer — Dry-ran a deterministic SOAR defensive playbook.
