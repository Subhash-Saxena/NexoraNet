# NexoraNet — Privacy & Ethical Analytics Policy

**“Learn. Simulate. Analyze. Defend.”**

## 1. Ethical Analytics Principles
1. **Educational Growth Orientation:** Analytics serve as diagnostic feedback to assist student learning, never as permanent summative labels.
2. **Neutral Phrasing:** Language is pedagogical and constructive (e.g. *"Recent practice suggests additional review of CIDR prefixes may be helpful"* rather than *"Deficient in networking"*).
3. **No Employment or Career Inferences:** The platform does not estimate employability, commercial readiness, or personality traits.
4. **No External Certification Claims:** Assessment reports explicitly state they document internal platform milestones, not accredited industry certifications.

## 2. Student Data Privacy
- **Default Confidentiality:** All student progress, assessment reports, and portfolios default to `PRIVATE`.
- **Public Portfolio Isolation:** Public portfolio views strictly filter out email addresses, password hashes, internal user IDs, and private instructor notes.
- **Answer Key & Flag Protection:** Student analytics and public portfolios never leak question answer keys, exam blueprints, or synthetic CTF flags.
- **Safe Hyperlinks:** Project URLs are strictly validated to prevent `javascript:` and `data:` URI attacks.
